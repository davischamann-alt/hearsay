    How Hearsay ranks places

    Nobody can pay to be here. Each spot is scored on three things:

    • Fit: does it match what you typed? We check its cuisine, its name, and what people said about it.
    • Word of mouth: how many independent voices recommend it. Local threads on Reddit, food blogs and newspaper or magazine coverage all count. One outlet repeating itself counts less than three different ones, and recent talk counts more than old talk.
    • Distance: a gentle nudge toward closer spots.

    Chains are left out. Delivery apps and ad-driven review sites (Yelp, Tripadvisor and the like) are never used as sources.
